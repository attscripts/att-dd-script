Sales Script · Existing AT&T Business Customers

Opening & Gatekeeper

Phase 1 · Identify the decision-maker

📋 Product Quick Facts — Know Before You Dial

  • Selling: Dynamic Defense Advanced — $65/mo with first 2 months FREE
  • Month-to-month, no long-term contract, cancel anytime
  • No new equipment, no technician visit, no downtime
  • Activates through customer's existing MyAT&T Business profile
  • Target: Existing AT&T Business Fiber customers
Say
Hi! / Good morning / Good afternoon, this is [AGENT NAME] calling regarding important security information for your AT&T internet service. Are you able to assist me?
IF NO Ask to speak with the Decision Maker (DM), and/or secure the DM name for a future callback.
IF YES Who am I speaking with? — capture name.
Say
Good morning / Afternoon [DM NAME]. My name is [AGENT NAME], an AT&T Solutions Provider calling on a recorded line.
COMPLIANCE: The recorded line disclosure is MANDATORY. Do NOT skip this. It must be stated clearly before continuing the call.
Once you reach the DM and the recorded line disclosure is done, proceed to Phase 2.

The Hook — Cyber Threat Context

Phase 2 · Set the stage with urgency
Say
The reason for my call is that cyber threats against businesses have increased significantly — especially attacks targeting point-of-sale systems, credit card terminals, and customer data. Small and mid-sized businesses are being hit particularly hard right now with ransomware and other threats.
TIP: Speak with urgency but stay calm and consultative. You're NOT trying to scare them — you're informing them. Pause briefly after this line to let it sink in.

Value Proposition — Dynamic Defense

Phase 3 · Introduce the product and the offer
Say
That's why AT&T created Dynamic Defense — a powerful new network-embedded security layer that uses advanced AI to detect and block threats in real time before they ever reach your network or devices.
Say · Price Anchor
AT&T wants you to experience the power of this AI security layer and is waiving the cost for the first two months. This means you get 2 months to try this network-embedded security risk-free. After two months, the service continues at only $65/month with no long-term contract, and you can cancel at any time.
TIP: Emphasize: "2 MONTHS FREE — RISK FREE." Let the price land. Don't rush into the close.
Say · Frictionless Setup
Dynamic Defense requires no new equipment, no technician visit, and no downtime. You simply activate it through your existing MyAT&T Business profile, and you get a clear dashboard for real-time visibility and control.

Closing — Authorization

Phase 4 · Confirm authority and capture verbal consent
Ask
Before we get you activated, quick question — are you the person who manages or has access to the MyAT&T Business profile for the account?
IF YES Perfect. Then let's go ahead and get your 2 months free of AT&T Dynamic Defense Advanced activated right now — sound good?
IF NO No problem — who handles that on your end? → Capture DM Name and schedule callback.
Ask · Verbal Authorization
Are you able to authorize us today to add Dynamic Defense to the account?
IF YES "Just to confirm on the recorded line, [OFFICE CONTACT NAME], you're authorizing AT&T to activate the Dynamic Defense Advanced 2-months free promotion for [BUSINESS NAME] today. Is that correct?"
IF NO No problem — when will be a good time to reach [DM NAME]? → Schedule callback.
COMPLIANCE: The verbal authorization above MUST be captured on the recorded line, word-for-word. This is your proof of consent. Wait for an audible "Yes" or "That's correct" before moving on.

Objection Handling

Phase 8 · Use these responses when customers push back

Top 10 Rebuttals

ObjectionRecommended Response
"I already have security / firewall / antivirus."Most businesses do. And that's why attackers are taking advantage of this. Dynamic Defense works before your firewall — it blocks threats at the network level using AT&T's AI and threat intelligence, so bad traffic never reaches your devices or firewall. It's an extra layer, not a replacement.
"It's too expensive / $65 is a lot."I understand. But $65/month is far less than the average cost of a data breach or ransomware incident for small businesses (often tens of thousands in downtime and recovery). Plus, you get the first two months completely free right now, so you can test it with zero risk.
"We don't need it / We haven't had any attacks."That's great to hear, but most businesses don't realize they've been targeted until something gets through. Attacks are up significantly, especially on POS systems and credit card data. Dynamic Defense stops them in real time before they hit your network — it's proactive protection instead of hoping nothing happens.
"I need to think about it / Talk to my IT guy."Absolutely, no pressure. Would it help if I send you a one-page overview with the key features? Or I can schedule a quick 5-minute call with you and your IT person to answer any technical questions.
"How do I know it won't slow down my internet?"It actually improves performance by blocking junk traffic before it reaches your network. It's embedded directly in AT&T's fiber network, so there's no extra hardware or latency added. Most customers see no difference in speed — sometimes even faster browsing.
"What if I want to cancel?"There's no long-term contract. You can cancel anytime with no termination fees. It just appears as a line item on your existing AT&T bill and can be removed easily through your MyAT&T Business portal.
"Is this only for big companies?"Not at all — this is specifically built for businesses like yours. AT&T took the same enterprise-grade protection used by large clients and made the Advanced package simple and affordable for small and mid-sized businesses on Business Fiber.
"I'm happy with my current provider."You don't need to switch providers. Dynamic Defense adds directly onto your existing AT&T Business Fiber service — no changes to your internet provider required.
"How much time does it take to set up?"Very little — usually under 30 minutes. No tech visit, no new equipment. You activate it through your existing MyAT&T Business dashboard and get instant visibility. I can walk you through it right now if you'd like.
"Can you prove it works?"Yes — customers are seeing blocked threats within the first few days. You'll have a real-time dashboard showing threats stopped at the network level. Plus, it uses the same AT&T threat intelligence that protects major enterprises.

Additional Rebuttals

ObjectionRecommended Response
"Our IT vendor handles security."Dynamic Defense complements your IT vendor by providing automated, up-to-date defenses and proactive reporting at the network level — before traffic even reaches their setup. Your IT vendor will appreciate the dashboard visibility.
"We're a small business; hackers won't target us."That's actually the most common misconception. Over 40% of cyberattacks target small businesses specifically — because attackers know smaller companies tend to have fewer defenses. Dynamic Defense protects against evolving threats regardless of company size.
"We're worried about complexity and disruption."Completely understandable. Dynamic Defense is designed for seamless integration — user-friendly controls, no install on your devices, and zero impact on daily operations. Most customers say they barely notice it's there except when they check the dashboard.
